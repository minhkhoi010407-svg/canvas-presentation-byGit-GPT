# Presentation QA Rubric

## Severity

- **P0 — blocking:** missing/corrupt slide, unreadable content, broken export, exposed confidential material, or materially false claim.
- **P1 — major:** broken storyline, unsupported core conclusion, wrong chart logic, missing asset, major crop, or severe layout failure.
- **P2 — visible:** overlap, clipping, weak contrast, inconsistent hierarchy, incorrect wrapping, alignment, source, or notation.
- **P3 — polish:** small optical or stylistic issue that does not impede comprehension.

## Release readiness

- **READY:** no P0–P2 issue remains; required formats and interactions pass.
- **READY WITH MINOR FIXES:** only P3 issues remain and the user accepts them.
- **NOT READY:** any P0/P1 issue remains, or P2 issues materially affect the live presentation.

## Storyline checks

- The deck establishes context quickly and makes the requested decision clear.
- Titles alone tell a coherent story.
- Evidence appears before or with the conclusion it supports.
- Repetition adds depth rather than restating the same point.
- The ending resolves the opening question and specifies next action.

## Evidence checks

- Sources, units, time periods, currencies, and definitions are explicit.
- Totals and percentages reconcile.
- Chart type and scale do not distort the message.
- Assumptions and scenarios are labeled.
- Claims are no stronger than their evidence.

## Visual checks

- A viewer can identify title, dominant proof, and takeaway within a few seconds.
- Body text is readable at normal presentation distance.
- Repeated cards, columns, and labels align consistently.
- No accidental overlap, overflow, clipping, or orphan line remains.
- Images use adequate resolution and deliberate crops.
- Color contrast and non-color cues support accessibility.

## File checks

- The intended fonts are available or safely substituted.
- External assets, videos, and links resolve on the delivery machine.
- PPTX and PDF exports match the approved state.
- Hidden slides, notes, comments, and metadata contain nothing unintended.
- The delivered filename and version are unambiguous.
