# Slide Visual Prompt Recipes

Adapt these structures to the approved deck style. Replace bracketed fields with concrete details; do not copy placeholders into a final prompt.

## Cover hero

```text
Wide presentation cover image expressing [governing idea]. [Primary subject] in [specific environment], [meaningful action or relationship]. Composition weighted to the [left/right], with calm negative space on the [opposite side] for a two-line title. [Medium and art direction], [lighting], [palette], [camera/lens], restrained detail, premium editorial finish. 16:9. No text, no watermark, no invented logos, no UI glyphs.
```

## Section divider

```text
Minimal visual metaphor for [section idea], one dominant subject, large areas of [background treatment], controlled [brand color] accent, low visual noise, strong silhouette, designed for a presentation section divider. 16:9 with safe margins. No text or watermark.
```

## Product-in-context scene

```text
[Product or supplied reference] used by [specific person or role] in [realistic context], showing [observable action] rather than generic posing. Preserve supplied product appearance exactly. Natural interaction, credible environment, [lighting and camera treatment], negative space reserved for annotations. No extra product variants, invented interface, text, or logo.
```

## Abstract system visual

```text
Conceptual illustration of [system idea] using [visual metaphor], with [number] clearly differentiated components and directional flow implied through composition, not labeled text. [Material or illustration style], [palette], clean background, presentation-safe geometry, wide composition. No typography, numbers, watermark, or fake interface.
```

## Editorial collage

```text
Editorial collage communicating [theme] through [2–4 specified motifs], unified by [paper/grain/light/material treatment]. One clear focal point, disciplined overlap, space for native slide labels, sophisticated magazine composition, [palette]. 16:9. No embedded text, logos, or watermarks.
```

## Transparent cutout asset

```text
Single isolated [object or subject], full silhouette visible, consistent [view angle], [lighting], and [material], clean edges, transparent background, no cast-off elements, no text, no watermark.
```

## Variant strategy

Change one dimension at a time:

- composition: left-weighted, centered, or panoramic;
- realism: photographic, editorial illustration, or dimensional 3D;
- energy: calm, active, or dramatic;
- abstraction: literal scene, symbolic object, or system metaphor.

Keep the slide message, brand palette, and placement constraints fixed so variants remain comparable.
