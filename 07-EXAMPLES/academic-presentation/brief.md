# Academic presentation brief (illustrative)

- **Topic:** Explain a peer-reviewed study to an interdisciplinary audience.
- **Audience shift:** Move from unfamiliarity to understanding the research question, method, result, and limits.
- **Evidence policy:** Use the paper and cited primary sources; do not extend results beyond the study population.
- **Output preference:** Editable-first PPTX with citations and notes.
- **Design direction:** High readability, restrained palette, method diagram, clearly labeled charts.
- **Unresolved inputs:** Study, time limit, audience expertise, and citation style must be supplied.
