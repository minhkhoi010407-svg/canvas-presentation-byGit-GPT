# Academic storyboard (template)

| Slide | Purpose / action title | Evidence needed | Visual intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The question matters because [verified context] | Primary-source context | Minimal title and one framing visual |
| 2 | Prior evidence leaves [specific gap] | Literature and gap source | Research map |
| 3 | The study tests [question] in [population] | Paper, inclusion criteria | Question and cohort |
| 4 | The method measures [construct] through [method] | Protocol and measure definitions | Flow diagram |
| 5 | The main result is [qualified finding] | Result table/figure, units | Faithful chart with denominator |
| 6 | The result does not establish [limit] | Study limitations | Boundary/uncertainty callout |
| 7 | The evidence supports [careful implication] | Findings plus context | Takeaway and discussion prompt |

Bracketed text is a placeholder, not a factual finding.
