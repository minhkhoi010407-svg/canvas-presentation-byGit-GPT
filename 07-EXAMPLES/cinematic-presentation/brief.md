# Cinematic presentation brief (illustrative)

- **Topic:** A story-led public talk with a strong visual throughline.
- **Audience shift:** Move the audience through a memorable narrative and one defensible closing idea.
- **Evidence policy:** Keep source notes for every factual scene or quotation; use metaphor without disguising it as evidence.
- **Output preference:** Image-first PDF/pages or hybrid deck, depending on accessibility/editability requirements.
- **Design direction:** Few words per slide, strong contrast, deliberate pacing, one focal image per beat.
- **Unresolved inputs:** Topic, image rights, screen format, speaker notes, and accessible text alternative.
