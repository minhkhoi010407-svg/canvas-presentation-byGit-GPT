# Cinematic storyboard (template)

| Beat | Audience effect | On-slide copy | Visual and transition intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Establish a question | One short question | One licensed/original focal image |
| Context | Make stakes concrete | One sourced fact | Spatial or scale contrast |
| Tension | Show a real constraint | One sentence | Visual narrowing or juxtaposition |
| Turn | Reveal new evidence | A precise claim | Change composition, not just color |
| Resolution | Connect evidence to meaning | One defensible takeaway | Return to opening motif with new context |
| Close | Invite a specific response | Clear next action | Quiet final frame and accessible text alternative |

All imagery and copy remain unspecified until researched and rights-checked.
