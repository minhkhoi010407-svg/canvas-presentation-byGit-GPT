# Corporate presentation brief (illustrative)

- **Topic:** Decision memo for a leadership team.
- **Audience shift:** Enable a specific decision, not a general tour of the topic.
- **Evidence policy:** Verify financial/operating metrics with dated internal sources; label estimates and scenarios.
- **Output preference:** Hybrid PPTX: editable titles, charts, and recommendation; licensed visuals only.
- **Design direction:** Decision-first titles, disciplined metric units, one primary chart per evidence slide.
- **Unresolved inputs:** Decision owner, alternatives, constraints, source data, and meeting duration.
