# Corporate storyboard (template)

| Slide | Purpose / action title | Evidence needed | Visual intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide whether to [specific action] by [date] | Decision context | Recommendation in one sentence |
| 2 | The current position creates [quantified issue] | Dated baseline and source | Baseline chart |
| 3 | Option A best fits [explicit criteria] | Comparable option evidence | Decision matrix |
| 4 | The expected outcome is [scenario range] | Assumptions and sensitivities | Range chart, no false precision |
| 5 | Delivery depends on [risks/mitigations] | Owners and validated risks | Milestone/risk view |
| 6 | Approve [action] with [guardrails] | Decision rights and dependencies | Ask, owner, next step |

Bracketed text is a placeholder, not a factual finding.
