# Data-driven presentation brief (illustrative)

- **Topic:** Explain a verified dataset to a decision-making audience.
- **Audience shift:** Understand a trend, its uncertainty, and a decision-relevant implication.
- **Evidence policy:** Preserve provenance, collection period, units, denominator, missingness, and transformation steps.
- **Output preference:** Editable-first PPTX for charts and text; provide the source data and chart definitions.
- **Design direction:** One question per chart, honest axis scales, accessible palette, direct labels where practical.
- **Unresolved inputs:** Dataset, data dictionary, audience, decision, privacy constraints, and output environment.
