# Data-driven storyboard (template)

| Slide | Question / action title | Data needed | Chart or visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What decision does this dataset inform? | Decision context | One-sentence framing |
| 2 | What was measured, when, and for whom? | Data dictionary, scope | Population/time overview |
| 3 | The observed measure changed from [x] to [y] | Cleaned data and method | Trend with units and dates |
| 4 | The change is concentrated in [segment] | Segment definitions and counts | Small multiples or grouped bars |
| 5 | [Uncertainty/limitation] affects interpretation | Missingness/intervals/confounders | Explicit uncertainty visual |
| 6 | The evidence supports [bounded action] | Verified results and options | Action with monitoring metric |

Bracketed values are placeholders. Validate calculations and data lineage before use.
